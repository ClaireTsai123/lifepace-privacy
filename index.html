Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Life Pace ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application ("App").

2. Information We Collect

With the addition of user login functionality powered by Amazon Cognito, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Authentication credentials (such as email address or username)
  • Session identifiers or tokens provided by Amazon Cognito

We do not collect or store passwords directly. Authentication is securely managed by Amazon Cognito.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate your account
  • Manage your login session
  • Provide account-based app functionality

We do not use your data for marketing, tracking, or sharing with third parties.

4. Local Data Storage

Your tasks, focus timer records, and progress information are stored locally on your device. This data is used solely to provide the core functionality of the app.

If you uninstall the app or clear app data, locally stored information may be deleted.

5. Data Security

We take your privacy seriously. Authentication is managed using Amazon Cognito, which provides security features including encryption and secure token management.

We do not store any sensitive personal data on our own servers.
